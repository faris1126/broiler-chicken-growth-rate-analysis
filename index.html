Broiler Chicken Growth Rate Analysis

Bioscience Problem

In Malaysia's poultry sector, monitoring weight is not enough. To optimize feed conversion efficiency, farmers must track the rate of change in body weight. This project uses derivatives to identify the "Critical Growth Phase" for Ross 308/Cobb broilers.

Mathematical Model

Weight Function: \[ f(x) = \frac{3.2}{1 + e^{-0.12(x-25)}} \] Derivative (Growth Rate): \[ f'(x) = \frac{0.384 e^{-0.12(x-25)}}{(1 + e^{-0.12(x-25)})^2} \]

Here, \( f(x) \) is body weight (kg) at age \( x \), and \( f'(x) \) is the instantaneous growth rate (kg/day).

Understanding Derivatives

A derivative represents the slope of the growth curve. If the weight is increasing, the derivative is positive. When the derivative reaches its peak, it identifies the day of maximum biological performance—a key metric for adjusting nutrient density in the diet.

Presentation Summary

  • Problem: Non-linear growth requires precision management.
  • Model: Gompertz/Logistic growth function models actual poultry behavior.
  • Application: \( f'(x) \) predicts when the flock requires peak caloric intake.
  • Farm Impact: Better timing leads to lower FCR and higher profit margins.
